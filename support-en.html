Nyanpo Alarm (喵步鬧鐘) Help & Support

A walking alarm where you raise pixel cats. The app’s interface is in Traditional Chinese; menu names below are shown in Chinese with an English gloss.

Why I made this app

Our cat at home passed away and became a cat angel. I wanted a place where I could always remember our cat, so I designed an app where everyone can set up their own beloved cat — so that, at least, we can keep remembering them and know they are doing okay.

I also hope that “The Wonderful Adventures of JoJo and Diandian (點顛)” can go on.

Contact

For questions, suggestions, or to report inappropriate content, email henrychao521@gmail.com (developer: Henry Chao). English or Chinese is fine. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (in “More → About”, 更多 → 關於).

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
  • Cat dialogue and room analysis use Apple Intelligence and need a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, the app uses fixed rules and templates; everything else works.
  • The DIY ESP32 pedometer is optional. You can try its screens in demo mode (示範模式).

FAQ

ProblemWhat to do
The alarm went off lateIn “More” (更多), make sure “Punctual mode” (準時模式) is on and location is allowed. With punctual mode off, the app estimates with motion sensors and may alert late.
Steps show 0 or don’t updateIn Settings → Privacy & Security, make sure “Motion & Fitness” and “Health” allow Nyanpo Alarm to read steps.
Can’t find friends nearbyBoth phones need “Cats follow nearby people home” (貓咪會跟著附近的人回家) turned on in “More”, with Local Network permission allowed, and must be close by (Bluetooth or the same Wi-Fi).
Someone nearby has an inappropriate nameGo to “More → Nearby people: block and report” (附近的人：封鎖與回報) to block them, or tap “Report” (回報) to email us.
A cat card QR code won’t readUse “More → Read a friend’s cat card” (讀取朋友的貓卡) and pick a photo where the whole QR code is sharp and visible.
“This iPhone doesn’t have Apple Intelligence” in the room analysisYour model doesn’t support it or it is turned off. Cats still act on temperature, time of day, and personality.
The pedometer keeps “searching”Make sure the pedometer’s Bluetooth is on and Wi-Fi is off (they can’t run at the same time), and its firmware is up to date (web flasher).
Will syncing to Health twice double my steps?No. When the same hour is synced again, Health replaces the old value with the new one.
